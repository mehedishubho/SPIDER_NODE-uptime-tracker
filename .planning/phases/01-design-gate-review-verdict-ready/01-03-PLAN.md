## Phase Goal

**As a** SpiderNode maintainer, **I want to** amend the design documents with every review addendum and flip the NOT READY verdict to READY, **so that** no correctness mechanism is ever invented under pressure during implementation.

**This plan's slice:** the failure-mode and cutover design (resilience + auth + connection budget) stands alone as a reviewable deliverable — a reader can answer "what happens when Redis/Postgres dies" and "how does every user log in after the flip" entirely from the paper.
